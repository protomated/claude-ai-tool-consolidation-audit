# Hartwell & Voss LLP — AI Tools We Currently Use

Rough list one of the partners put together before the audit. Fictional firm and fictional tool names — synthetic test data only.

- **QuickBrief AI** — a browser-based AI writing assistant. The associates use it for drafting client emails and for quick research summaries when we don't have time to read a full case. It's the free version. We sometimes type a client's name into it when drafting a reply.
- **Clio Manage** — our practice management system. Business plan, we signed their data processing agreement when we onboarded. Handles matter management, billing, and calendaring for the whole firm.
- **DeadlineGuard** — a dedicated court-deadline and docketing tool. Calculates filing deadlines off the court rules for our jurisdictions. We're on their firm plan and signed a DPA with them too.
- **NotesApp Free** — two of our paralegals use this free note-taking app on their phones to jot down call notes during client intake calls, including the caller's name and what the call was about.
- **SummarizeIt** — another AI tool, free tier, that one of the associates started using for the same kind of research summaries QuickBrief AI does. Only used for case-law research, not client correspondence. Not sure what their data policy is — nobody's checked.
- **TranscribeFast** — we use this for transcribing depositions. We're on their paid business tier. Honestly not sure if we ever signed anything about how they use the audio afterward — that predates me.
- **MarketingBot** — generates social media captions for our firm's Instagram. Free tier. Never touches anything client-related, just general marketing copy about the firm.

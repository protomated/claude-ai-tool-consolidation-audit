# VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Vendor Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between **Castellane & Ruiz LLP** ("Client") and **Ferrowave Cloud Solutions, Inc.** ("Vendor").

## 1. Limitation of Liability

Vendor's total liability arising out of this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid in the three (3) months preceding the claim. Client's liability under this Agreement is uncapped.

## 2. Indemnification

Each party shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the other party from third-party claims arising from (a) the indemnifying party's breach of this Agreement, (b) infringement of intellectual property rights, or (c) the indemnifying party's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

## 3. Termination

Vendor may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon ten (10) days' prior written notice to Client. Client may terminate only for Vendor's material breach that remains uncured five (5) days after written notice.

## 4. Confidentiality

Each party's confidentiality obligations shall survive for five (5) years following termination or expiration of this Agreement, except that obligations with respect to trade secrets shall survive for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law. This section does not apply to information that is public, independently developed, rightfully received from a third party, or required to be disclosed by law.

## 5. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be resolved exclusively through binding arbitration administered in Singapore, with each party bearing its own costs and travel expenses regardless of outcome.

## 6. Payment Terms

Payment is due within forty-five (45) days of the invoice date. Any good-faith dispute over an invoiced amount must be raised in writing within fifteen (15) days of receipt; undisputed amounts remain due on the original schedule.

## 7. Data Security and Breach Notification

Vendor shall notify Client in writing within ten (10) business days of discovering any unauthorized access to or disclosure of Client's data.

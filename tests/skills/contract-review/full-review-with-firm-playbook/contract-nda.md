# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between **Meridian Bookkeeping Solutions LLC** and **Harlow & Voss Consulting Group** for the purpose of evaluating a potential business relationship.

## 1. Confidentiality

Each party's confidentiality obligations shall survive for three (3) years following disclosure. This section does not apply to information that is public, independently developed, rightfully received from a third party, or required to be disclosed by law.

## 2. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, and any dispute shall be resolved in the state or federal courts located in Delaware.

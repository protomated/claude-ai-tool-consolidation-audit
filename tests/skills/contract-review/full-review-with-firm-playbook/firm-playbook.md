# Meridian Bookkeeping Solutions LLC — Contract Playbook

Firm-specific negotiation positions. Supersedes the plugin's bundled generic playbook wherever a clause type below is addressed.

---

## Limitation of Liability

**Firm position (🟢):** Liability capped at six (6) months of fees paid, mutual, with carve-outs for confidentiality breach, IP infringement, and gross negligence/willful misconduct.

**Acceptable fallback (🟡):** A cap between six (6) and twelve (12) months of fees, provided it remains mutual.

**Red flags (🔴):** No cap at all; a cap above twelve (12) months of fees; a cap that is one-directional.

**Suggested redline language:** "Each party's total liability shall not exceed the fees paid or payable in the six (6) months preceding the claim, except for breaches of confidentiality, IP infringement, or gross negligence/willful misconduct."

---

## Indemnification

**Firm position (🟢):** Mutual, scoped to third-party claims from breach, IP infringement, or gross negligence/willful misconduct.

**Acceptable fallback (🟡):** One-directional if Meridian is clearly the lower-risk party and scope is limited to IP infringement and confidentiality breach.

**Red flags (🔴):** Uncapped indemnification obligation on Meridian; scope broader than third-party claims.

**Suggested redline language:** "Each party shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the other party from third-party claims arising from (a) the indemnifying party's breach of this Agreement, (b) infringement of intellectual property rights, or (c) the indemnifying party's gross negligence or willful misconduct, subject to the limitation of liability set forth in Section [__]."

---

## Termination

**Firm position (🟢):** Mutual termination for material breach with a thirty (30) day cure period, and mutual termination for convenience with thirty (30) days' notice.

**Acceptable fallback (🟡):** Longer notice or cure periods.

**Red flags (🔴):** Only the counterparty has a termination-for-convenience right; no cure period for material breach.

**Suggested redline language:** "Either party may terminate this Agreement (a) for material breach by the other party that remains uncured thirty (30) days after written notice, or (b) for convenience upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other party."

---

## Confidentiality / Non-Disclosure

**Firm position (🟢):** Mutual, standard carve-outs, five (5) year survival (indefinite for trade secrets).

**Acceptable fallback (🟡):** Shorter survival period for low-sensitivity engagements.

**Red flags (🔴):** One-directional obligation; no carve-outs; no time limit at all.

**Suggested redline language:** "Each party's confidentiality obligations under this Agreement shall survive for five (5) years following termination or expiration of this Agreement, except that obligations with respect to trade secrets shall survive for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law."

---

## Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

**Firm position (🟢):** Litigation (not arbitration) in Meridian's home state courts (Delaware) or a neutral state both parties agree to.

**Acceptable fallback (🟡):** None — Meridian's leadership has directed that mandatory arbitration is never acceptable, regardless of venue or cost-shifting terms, following a prior dispute where arbitration costs exceeded the underlying claim.

**Red flags (🔴):** Any mandatory arbitration clause, regardless of forum or cost allocation; venue outside Delaware or a mutually agreed neutral state.

**Suggested redline language:** "This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, and any dispute shall be resolved exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Delaware. The parties waive any right to compel arbitration of a dispute arising under this Agreement."

---

## Payment Terms

**Firm position (🟢):** Net 15, with a defined invoice-dispute process.

**Acceptable fallback (🟡):** Net 16 through Net 30, provided a dispute process is defined.

**Red flags (🔴):** Net 31 or longer; no defined dispute process; late payment triggering automatic default or termination.

**Suggested redline language:** "Payment is due within fifteen (15) days of the invoice date. Any good-faith dispute over an invoiced amount must be raised in writing within fifteen (15) days of receipt; undisputed amounts remain due on the original schedule."

---

## Intellectual Property Assignment / License

**Firm position (🟢):** Pre-existing IP stays with its creator; only IP specifically developed for and paid for under the agreement is assigned.

**Acceptable fallback (🟡):** A license instead of full assignment, if that meets the counterparty's actual need.

**Red flags (🔴):** Blanket assignment of all IP created during the contract term regardless of relation to the engagement; no carve-out for Meridian's pre-existing tools and templates.

**Suggested redline language:** "Each party retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property. Intellectual property specifically developed for and paid for under this Agreement is assigned to Meridian upon full payment; each party grants the other a non-exclusive, limited license to use any pre-existing IP solely as necessary to perform or receive the benefit of this Agreement."

---

## Data Security & Breach Notification

**Firm position (🟢):** Vendor notifies Meridian of any security incident affecting Meridian's data within seventy-two (72) hours of discovery, and specifies its data-encryption and access-control practices.

**Acceptable fallback (🟡):** Notification within seventy-three (73) to one hundred twenty (120) hours.

**Red flags (🔴):** No breach-notification obligation at all; notification window longer than one hundred twenty (120) hours; no mention of encryption or access controls for data at rest or in transit.

**Suggested redline language:** "Vendor shall notify Meridian in writing within seventy-two (72) hours of discovering any unauthorized access to or disclosure of Meridian's data, and shall maintain industry-standard encryption for data at rest and in transit."

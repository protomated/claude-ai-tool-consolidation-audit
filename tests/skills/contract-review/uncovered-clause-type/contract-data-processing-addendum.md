# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM AND SERVICE TERMS

This Data Processing Addendum and Service Terms ("Agreement") is entered into between **Okafor & Bly Immigration Law** ("Client") and **Streamline Docket Systems, Inc.** ("Vendor").

## 1. Limitation of Liability

Each party's total liability arising out of this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim, except for breaches of confidentiality, infringement of intellectual property rights, or claims arising from gross negligence or willful misconduct.

## 2. Confidentiality

Each party's confidentiality obligations shall survive for five (5) years following termination or expiration of this Agreement. This section does not apply to information that is public, independently developed, rightfully received from a third party, or required to be disclosed by law.

## 3. Service Level Commitment

Vendor commits to 99.5% platform uptime, measured monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance windows. Client is entitled to service credits, calculated per the fee schedule in Exhibit A, for any month in which uptime falls below this commitment.

## 4. Data Security and Breach Notification

Vendor shall notify Client in writing within seventy-two (72) hours of discovering any unauthorized access to or disclosure of Client's data, and shall maintain SOC 2 Type II certification for the duration of this Agreement.

## 5. Sub-Processor Approval

Vendor shall not engage a new sub-processor to handle Client's data without providing Client thirty (30) days' advance written notice and a reasonable opportunity to object.

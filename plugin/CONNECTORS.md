# Connectors

This plugin requires no connectors. It operates entirely within your Claude Desktop conversation — no external services are accessed, no credentials are needed, and no data is transmitted outside the conversation.

The plugin drafts billing narratives from the notes you provide and presents them for your review. You copy the final narrative and paste it into your billing system (Clio, MyCase, PracticePanther, or similar). No integration, no authorization step.

## Privacy note

The plugin processes your notes within your Claude Desktop conversation under your Claude plan's data handling terms. No matter details, client names, or narrative drafts are transmitted to Protomated or any third party. You control what information you paste into the conversation.

For confidential matter information: confirm you are on Claude for Work, Claude Team, or Claude Enterprise before using this plugin with client details. See the main README for plan requirements.
